About our group

The Particles and Fields group at the State University of Londrina works at the interface between quantum field theory (QFT) and condensed matter. Its research has been published in leading journals, including the Journal of High Energy Physics, Physical Review B, Physical Review D, and Physical Review Letters. The group focuses on non-perturbative methods in QFT, including the 1/N expansion, bosonization, conformal invariance, dualities, and topological field theories. These versatile tools help describe important phenomena such as phase transitions and topological order. The group collaborates with researchers at Boston University (USA) and the Paul Scherrer Institute (Switzerland), providing opportunities for its students to undertake doctoral research visits abroad.
